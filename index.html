Our story

A home-style silog, plated the Japanese way

The Filipino silog — savory marinated meat, garlic rice, and a perfect egg — is breakfast, comfort, and celebration all at once. At Silogan ni Soma we honor that tradition and reimagine it through Japanese technique: donburi bowls, dashi-rich broths, precise knifework, and ingredients treated with quiet respect. Two food cultures, one warm table.
